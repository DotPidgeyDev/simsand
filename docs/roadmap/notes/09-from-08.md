# Milestone 09 notes, from closing Milestone 08

**Written:** 2026-11-27, closing Milestone 08  
**Use when:** starting society and world depth

## What the world already does

A living character picks one action from the scored definitions in
`packages/simulation/src/systems/actions.ts`, from `idle` through
`buy`. Commitment is five seconds. An urgent need at 0.8 interrupts and
cools the cut-off action for 20 seconds. The shared route field seeks
water, land, camps, and piles. Social actions and trade start only
beside someone.

The log is one record per fact, indexed by participant and pair, with
three kinds of event:

- Action events: completed social and economy actions, and failed or
  interrupted endings.
- Intervention events: bless, curse, bounty, and gift, each naming the
  command, the issuer, and for the director a reason.
- Lifecycle events: `character.died`, with the cause (`old_age`) and
  age. The lifecycle system writes it once, on the tick a character
  reaches their lifespan.

`isActionLogEvent`, `isInterventionLogEvent`, and `isLifecycleLogEvent`
in `@simsand/shared` tell them apart.

The chronicle scores that log into tiers. It is never a second store.
Notable entries reach the browser feed, significant ones pulse a ring,
and dramatic ones (bless, curse, death) take camera focus.

The world director in `packages/simulation/src/director/` issues the
same intervention commands as `system`, with a reason. Its rules are
mercy, scarcity, kindness, and hubris. It acts at most once a minute,
and on one character at most once in 5 minutes.

A pile site is any tile that holds a pile or is waiting for one to
return. A bounty on bare grass starts a new site. A depleted site
returns to stock 3 after 90 seconds.

Saves are format version 25. The database schema is version 6.

Regression for Milestones 03–08 is in
`packages/simulation/src/regression.test.ts`.

## What Milestone 09 will find in the way

1. **The three open rules come first.** The roadmap lists them under
   Milestone 09: a route that seeks a person or a stack, what happens
   to a dead holder's stacks, and a declared preference when two jobs
   share a score. Combat, trade across the map, and families each need
   at least one of them.

2. **Death is now a fact to hang rules on.** A dead holder's stacks can
   move, stay on the body, or vanish on the `character.died` event,
   instead of on a lifecycle check every tick. The dead stay in the
   world as an X, still holding their stacks, and nothing replaces them.
   Families and generations start from that.

3. **Causes of death are an append-only catalog.** `DEATH_CAUSES` is
   `['old_age']`. Combat, starvation, or wildlife append a cause. The
   chronicle summary names only the recorded cause, so a new cause
   needs its own wording in `DIED_OF`, not an invented motive.

4. **New world facts that are not actions or commands are lifecycle
   events.** A birth, a settlement founded, or a faction formed fits
   the third kind better than a fake action ending. The server
   validates each lifecycle type's payload on save
   (`assertLifecyclePayload`), so a new type needs a check there.

5. **Code that reads action fields must check the kind first.** The
   director's kindness rule reads `payload.result` and `initiator` only
   after `isActionLogEvent`. Any new rule that walks the log does the
   same.

6. **Quests and special encounters moved here from Milestone 08.**
   Like interventions, each should be a recorded fact the chronicle can
   score. Whether the director issues them is still open. If a quest
   lasts across ticks, its state is saved, which means a save format
   bump.

7. **The director reacts. It does not plan.** It must not choose what a
   character does. A profession, faction, or settlement that needs
   steering gets a simulation rule, not a director rule.

8. **Stubs to leave alone until a system reads them.** Aggression and
   courage wait for conflict. Ambition waits for goals. The belonging
   need waits until the existing social actions are not enough.

## Persistence, inspection, and test constraints

- Catalogs are append-only: action ids, log types, memory kinds, items,
  death causes, director reasons, and chronicle tiers. Regression pins
  the order of the action ids, log types, memory kinds, items, and
  director reasons.
- Component arrays are process-global. A second `createSimulationWorld`
  in the same test overwrites the first world's slots.
- Anything Milestone 09 saves bumps the save format past 25 and needs
  an upgrade path from 25. Load validation lives in
  `apps/server/src/world-store.ts`.
- Director settings are server config, not part of the save. A new
  rule needs a `SIMSAND_DIRECTOR_RULES` name and its own overrides.
- Tick performance is a Milestone 10 concern. At 1,000 entities the
  pure tick was about 4–5 ms against a 10 ms guardrail. A system that
  scans every character pair, or the whole log, each tick will blow
  that. Use the participant and pair indexes.

## Suggested first increment

Decide what happens to a dead holder's stacks, and apply it on
`character.died`. It is the smallest of the three open rules, it reuses
the event this milestone just added, and families and inheritance
depend on it.
