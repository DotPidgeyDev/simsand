# Milestone 06 notes, from closing Milestone 05

**Written:** 2026-11-21, closing Milestone 05  
**Use when:** starting durable world persistence and indexes

## What the world already does

A living character picks one action from scored definitions in
`packages/simulation/src/systems/actions.ts`. The catalog is `idle`,
`move`, `eat`, `rest`, `drink`, `work`, `greet`, `comfort`, `keep`,
`confide`, `bluff`, `give`, `gather`, `craft`, `dine`, `buy`. Commitment
is five seconds. An urgent need at 0.8 interrupts and cools the cut-off
action for 20 seconds. Finishing a meal, drink, rest, or visit does not
cool it.

Items are `goods`, `material`, and `food`. A holder with nothing has no
inventory record. A pile with nothing is not in `world.nodes`.

A completed camp visit with empty hands adds one goods. Holding one
material at a camp spends it and adds one food. Standing on a pile,
gather moves one material from the pile into the hand. An industrious
character walks to the nearer of a camp and a pile. A tie keeps the
camp. One food, eaten on land, brings hunger down to the quiet line.
The meal with no food is unchanged and still quiets hunger. A character
beside someone they have already met gives one goods when they are not
greedy, or pays two goods for one food when they are. The price is
`FOOD_PRICE`. Neither transfer counts as another meeting. One log event
covers the pair. The first gift is one `giving` memory. The first
purchase is one `trading` memory.

A pile that reaches zero is scheduled on `world.returning` and comes
back on the same tile at stock 3 after 90 seconds. That delay is longer
than the 75 seconds hunger takes to rise from satisfied to the quiet
line at the default rate. Until the pile returns it is not a route
target. `renewPiles` runs at the start of the tick from
`world.elapsedSeconds`. Work does not spend material. Nothing seeks a
person or a stack that is not already beside the actor.

Greed is read only by `give` and `buy`. Aggression, courage, and
ambition are still unread. Needs are still hunger, thirst, and fatigue.
Death leaves a corpse and its stacks in place. Removing the character
deletes the inventory. Neighbours cannot take those stacks.

Saves are format version 23. A version 22 save gains an empty return
list. Older saves still upgrade, one version at a time. A gathered pile
that was already gone in an old save does not come back: the timer
starts only when a pile is emptied after the upgrade. `ACTION_IDS`,
`MEMORY_KINDS`, `SIMULATION_LOG_TYPES`, and `ITEM_IDS` are append-only.
Component arrays are process-global: a second `createSimulationWorld`
in the same test overwrites the first world's slots.

The log, memories, and relationships are world-level lists. Inspection
reads them through indexes built when the record is written. The render
delta carries position,
terrain, and the resting, eating, drinking, working, and lifecycle
flags. It does not carry inventories or piles. Inspection does.

Regression for Milestones 03, 04, and 05 is in
`packages/simulation/src/regression.test.ts`.

## What Milestone 06 will find in the way

1. **The save already holds the economy.** Inventories, piles, and the
   return list are on `PersistedWorld`. The next field is another
   version. Do not store a waiting pile as a node with quantity 0.
   Invariants reject that, and the route treats every node as a place
   to walk.

2. **The live log is an array, capped at 10,000.** Gifts and purchases
   are on it, one event for both participants. The participant and pair
   indexes are built in `history/records.ts` when the event, memory, or
   relationship is written, and rebuilt on restore. Do not copy the
   event onto each character. Events that fall off the cap wait on
   `pendingLog` until the server confirms they are in `simulation_events`.
   That table keeps the newest 100,000 of the same records. A crash
   before the next save still loses the unconfirmed ones.

3. **A jump in elapsed time returns every due pile at once.** The
   return is `world.elapsedSeconds >= renewsAt`. Setting the clock
   forward also ages hunger and fatigue and will mint goods from every
   camp visit that completes along the way. Restart does not do that.
   See item 7. Do not add a catch-up to clear a pile that is waiting.

4. **The free visit and the free meal are closed.** Empty hands at a
   camp still yield one goods. Hunger still falls with no food. Do not
   remove either to make the economy mandatory.

5. **Deltas are not the save.** Showing stacks and piles on the map is
   Milestone 08. A route to a person or a stack, a dead holder's
   stacks, and a declared job preference are Milestone 09.

6. **Do not scan the log or memories every tick.** The same rule that
   kept social actions off an all-pairs matrix applies to the index:
   build it when an event is written, and read it by key.

   **Done (2026-11-21).** `historyFor`, `historyForPair`, `memoriesFor`,
   and `relationshipsFor` read the indexes. The same-tick pair check
   reads that pair's list. Restore calls `rebuildHistoryIndexes`.

   **Retained (2026-11-21).** Schema version 2 stores each log event once.
   Participant and pair rows point at `event_id`. Inspection merges that
   store with the live window. `acknowledgePendingLog` drops only the
   events the save confirmed.

7. **Offline time is not simulated.** A restart resumes at the saved
   tick and elapsed time. Wall-clock downtime does not age hunger,
   return piles, or finish camp visits. The loop's catch-up only covers
   ticks the running process fell behind on, and it stays bounded.
   Fast-forward is a later time-scaling control. Do not add a restart
   jump to clear a pile that is waiting.
